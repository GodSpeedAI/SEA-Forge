## Findings requiring correction

1. **Verified-empty eligibility is broader than the operator-approved predicate.** The approval receipt requires an “actual registered absent/zero-byte readable stream under the cooperative lock.” Current spec `REQ-C2-RANGE-005` says “A readable stream proven absent or zero-byte under the cooperative lock” and its error clause covers corrupt/unreadable history; ADR-008 says “a proven readable absent or zero-byte stream.” Neither requires that the stream be registered. This leaves a path to classify an unregistered/missing stream as successful empty, which the approved predicate does not authorize. State the registration requirement consistently in the spec, acceptance vector, and ADR.

2. **The signed exact-byte bindings do not specify the approved digest construction.** Proposal revision 3 and the revision-4 approval require a domain-separated SHA-256 binding over a presence tag, checked fixed-width UTF-8 byte length, and exact bytes for stream identity and each cursor filter. Current `REQ-C2-RANGE-005` says signed bindings “MUST encode presence, checked byte length, and exact bytes”; it does not say these are domain-separated digests, and may be read as requiring raw filter values in the token. `REQ-C2-RANGE-004` requires exact stream binding but likewise omits the specified byte-binding construction. ADR-008 repeats the ambiguity (“bindings include presence, checked byte length, and exact bytes”). State the digest construction and comparison to the target stream/filter bytes without requiring raw strings in the token; retain the existing token/payload caps and no-ID-grammar rule.

3. **Seed erasure and entropy-failure verification are not normative/test-traceable.** The approved proposal’s key setup uses existing `getrandom::fill`, constructs the existing signing key, and clears the local seed with existing `zeroize`; `REQ-C2-RANGE-004` requires a fresh key and fail-closed entropy but does not require clearing the temporary seed. `V-C2-RANGE-01` covers key freshness/restart rejection, but not startup failure on entropy failure or local-seed clearing. Add the seed-clearing requirement and an acceptance case for entropy failure; preserve the no-new-dependency decision.

## Confirmed alignment and limits

The spec and ADR retain the previous six-area C2 contract and its readiness gates. The reader additions correctly retain legacy vector/EventFrame shape, separate v2 DTOs, typed `entry_hash` and complete-`Value` `payload_hash`, tolerated/ignored unknown top-level fields, hashed unknown payload fields, known event-field checks including nullable `detail`, and the no-new-kind-grammar rule (`REQ-C2-RANGE-002..003`; ADR-008 “Bounded reader…”). The four approved policy choices are separated in metadata and the ADR, without claiming exact-hash approval. The raw row/page caps are additive while the 500-row/500-frame/1 MiB response/50 ms lock caps remain unchanged (`limits.event_page`; `REQ-C2-RANGE-004`; ADR-008 resource-limits section). Filter resolution is signed and limited to acknowledged validated rows; ordinal zero, unresolved bounds, empty-bound refusal, and lookahead non-acknowledgement are represented (`REQ-C2-RANGE-005`, `V-C2-RANGE-02..03`). The separate two-page/500 ms owner scheduling limit is not recast as a reader I/O deadline (`REQ-C2-RANGE-002`, `V-C2-RANGE-03`; ADR-008).

No source, schema, test, compiler, gate, runtime, status, or Git action was performed. These findings do not change approval of the four operator-selected policy areas; they identify specification fidelity and acceptance-traceability gaps that should be corrected before accepting this normative package.
