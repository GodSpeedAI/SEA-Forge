## Revision 4 findings addressed

1. **Reappearance across bounded-window eviction:** revision 5's exact `SeenByID` ledger and monotone first-observation ordinal, scoped to the live `(case_id, run_id)` entry and never evicted during that entry's lifetime, prevent an already accepted identity from becoming new merely because it leaves/re-enters the current 1,024-frame window (proposal lines 218-285). IDs remain opaque exact text; ordinal order comes from first appearance in accepted source-response order, not sorting or parsing IDs. This is consistent with the source recon: one uninterrupted successful recorder emits sequential IDs, but the public source does not establish unconditional append-only continuity. Revision 5 correctly limits its guarantee to registry-entry lifetime, allows a later new entry/cohort to observe IDs again, keeps source-unseen loss unknown, and does not infer loss from `TotalFrameCount` or ordinal differences.
2. **Initial helper-pruned frames:** each lease high-water mark is initialized from the captured source version before calling the accepted initial DTO helper (lines 261-268,566-574). Thus intentionally pruned initial frames are not mislabeled as future gaps or replayed by the lease. Later exact eviction reporting is limited to ledger IDs whose ordinal is above that lease's prior high-water and that are absent from the captured current window (lines 270-285,586-592). The document correctly separates known observed-and-evicted IDs from source rows never observed and prohibits a fabricated total.
3. **Busy global-generation retry:** `Next` is serialized per lease, captures one immutable version set, builds off-lock, rechecks authorization/context, and commits only that lease's watermarks for the captured versions. It does not globally retry until every poller is latest. If an entry advanced, a later wake is preserved; the returned delta is explicitly as-of its captured versions (lines 28-34,533-550). This is the root-directed progress model and avoids the revision 4 starvation loop without claiming continuous latest state.
4. **Root exhaustion interpretation:** the proposal now sets `Exhausted=(ReadsAttempted==8 && R>8)`, makes cached/shared reuse and fewer than eight actual logical calls false, and records `R==8` false, while preserving that the approved schema wording is causally ambiguous (lines 69-92). This matches root's executor decision; it is not represented as a schema clarification or operator amendment.
5. **Guard source and initial DTO:** it describes the implemented guard as current private source and as-of only, and the accepted DTO/helper remain exact (lines 94-123,494-505,699-708). The existing helper deep-copies, caps the exact JSON DTO, prunes deterministically, and rejects only irreducible metadata overflow (`run_observation_hydration_cap.go:22-78,80-149`). Revision 5 preserves the previous revision's accepted selector/count/read ownership rules and failed-`Prepare` rollback outline.

## Blocking contract omissions and remaining source-release holds

### 1. No cohort/lease admission bound is present in revision 5

Revision 4 expressly kept a **proposal-only maximum of 16 cohort leases**, at up to eight run refs each (128 attachments), separate from the accepted 16-poller registry (`run-observation-manager-concrete-proposal-revision4-oct06.md:24-32,499-510`). Revision 5 removes that policy from its concrete bounds and `RunObservationManager` type: `entries` is capped at 16, but `leases` at lines 455-465 has no count limit, and `Prepare` can allocate another lease/watermark/wake/operation state while attaching to already-running pollers. The initial assignment accepts the shared 16-poller limit; it does not make the number of cohorts or refs finite.

Without an explicit decision, many leases can attach to the same 16 keys, so per-lease maps, wake channels, notifier/ref state, pending operations, and authorization checks can grow without a manager-level bound. A statement that the 1 MiB budget is per poller does not cover this cohort-owned state. Add the maximum-cohort admission proposal, count preparing/active/draining leases, define its typed no-list/no-read refusal and slot-release conditions, and show the resulting maximum attachment count (16 x 8 = 128). Keep it **proposal-only pending independent review and operator approval**. Neither 16 pollers nor the new cohort cap should be silently treated as a heap/OOM bound.

### 2. The per-poller budget and its new state transitions remain proposed policy

Revision 5 gives a detailed deterministic 1 MiB combined serialized budget for the exact ever-seen ledger, retained safe frames and auxiliary value metadata, and defines atomic overflow/recovery/unavailable behavior (lines 17-26,295-353). This is a useful, testable proposal. It is not in the original accepted 16-poller/1,024-window contract, and revision 4 correctly required independent review and operator approval before source work. Revision 5 itself labels the budget and overflow/terminal policy as unapproved (lines 297-302,344-353,747-756); retain that status. The source task must not proceed on the assumption that the limit or its semantics were accepted.

The serialized JSON image is explicitly not a Go heap or process-RSS guarantee, and excludes decoded in-flight responses/transport buffers/control state (lines 304-327). The 16-poller maximum times a 1 MiB serialized-value policy would describe at most 16 MiB of those serialized retained values only, if both policies are separately accepted. The initial 1 MiB DTO cap is distinct. No aggregate process-memory claim follows.

### 3. `Next` omits a typed error for the declared poller read-failure state

The error inventory at lines 210-216 lists `ErrPollerRetentionUnavailable`, but not `ErrPollerReadUnavailable`. The declared enum has `pollerReadUnavailable` (`:395-415`), `pollerAvailability` has the same state (`:365-370`), and the worker prose says a failed `ReadRunTrace` makes `Next` return a typed unavailable error rather than serving the previous version as current (`:523-530`). This leaves the exact `Next` result contract undefined for a required path. Add the typed read-unavailable error to the enumerated API and table, with zero delta/no watermark advance and recovery semantics, or revise the state/outcome consistently. Do not make an old buffer look current after a newer failed read.

### 4. Version capture time versus first-ever poller time should be made unambiguous at DTO construction

Revision 5 correctly defines `pollerVersion.ObservedAt` as the capture time of that accepted source version and says a `RunDelta` names its captured version's time (lines 117-123,142-148,157-165). It also retains `pollerEntry.firstAcceptedAt` as an immutable first-read time in its state and serialized budget (lines 308-311,424-440). State explicitly that each initial run DTO's `ObservedAt` is copied from the exact captured `pollerVersion.ObservedAt`; reuse preserves **that version's** timestamp, while a subsequent accepted poll gets its own capture timestamp. `firstAcceptedAt` may remain only if its separate purpose is defined and it cannot be used as a later version's observation time. Add cache-reuse and changed-version timestamp fixtures. This avoids reintroducing the stale “first-ever poller time” semantics that revision 4 had.

## Ownership and candidate-version clarity required before source assignment

Revision 5 materially improves concrete ownership: `pollerEntry` names worker cancel/done and `notifierRefs`; `cohortLease` names refs, scalar watermarks, wake/stop channels, single-Next token, identity and operation wait group; rollback retains draining capacity and joins work outside the lock (lines 355-479,507-564). It also says poller versions are immutable and swapped atomically. Before source work, specify two invariants the structs currently leave implicit:

- `pollerVersion.Snapshot` itself contains a mutable `Frames` slice and frame optional pointers, while `pollerVersion.Frames` separately contains frame values plus ordinals (`ports.RunTraceSnapshot` has `Frames []RunTraceFrame` and pointer-valued fields at `internal/ports/run_trace.go:13-29`). Define whether these are one owned representation or deliberate copies; deep-copy the slice and `ExecutionStatus`/`ExitCode` pointers when accepting a source read; ensure no reader or builder can mutate a committed version. Count every retained value exactly once in the serialized image, or explain any duplicate storage.
- `notifierRefs` is an entry-level count but notifications target each lease's `wake` channel. The prose properly requires acquiring refs before unlock, sending/releasing outside lock, and preventing channel close until outstanding refs drain (`:551-564`). Specify that each acquired ref is tied to the target lease/channel (or that entry-wide waiting intentionally delays all affected closes), which owner releases it on every path, and how a lease detach waits without holding the manager lock. Keep no send/cancel/wait/join under the lock.

These are implementation-contract clarification, not a reason to weaken the required lifecycle. Preserve the original assignment's failed-`Prepare` ownership: mark provisional lease draining, prohibit new refs, detach every acquired ref exactly once, cancel only pollers with no other authorized watcher, join owned list/read/initializer/notifier work outside locks, and free capacity only after actual read/client/worker completion. A failed `Prepare` returns no lease for caller cleanup. Revision 5's rollback prose correctly preserves these requirements (lines 507-564), but focused tests must prove each rollback boundary, partial-attach case, owner/waiter initializer race, concurrent `Stop`, and timeout remains draining.

## Verdict and next revision

Revision 5 substantively repairs the revision 4 watermark and `Next` starvation findings, corrects the stale guard/error contract, and uses root's exhaustion decision with the ambiguity caveat. It is a major architecture improvement, but it still omits the proposal-only cohort admission cap, does not list the declared poller-read error in `Next`, and needs the current-version timestamp and immutable-buffer/notifier ownership invariants made explicit. In addition, the 16-cohort cap, combined 1 MiB per-poller budget, and overflow/recovery policies must remain unapproved until their own independent review and operator decision.

**Reject as a complete manager implementation blueprint for now; no source task is released.** Preserve revision 5 and this immutable review. A fresh revision should close the private contract gaps and explicitly enumerate all proposed caps and transition policies without implying their approval. The exhaustion formula itself now follows root's executor interpretation with its schema-ambiguity caveat; admission-cap and retention-policy approval remain separate prerequisites for any source release.
