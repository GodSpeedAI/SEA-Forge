# GodSpeed AI glossary map

Start with the vocabulary of the context that owns the concept. CognitiveOS owns the navigation ontology. DomainForge and CEP own their declared semantic and exchange contracts. A product may realize part of that ontology, carry a reference into another context, or supply evidence for a later judgment.

## Relationships

- **CognitiveOS → Horizon.** Horizon projects a purposeful case into the current decision and its assessed choices.
- **DomainForge → CEP → consumers.** CEP carries DomainForge semantic identity and profile-specific meaning across product boundaries. Consumers maintain their own execution, projection, and evidence identities.
- **Horizon and SEA-Forge → Cognate Workbench and Cognate.** Handoffs can carry a case reference and, when bound by the case authority, a Case Assignment. Workbench Expert Assignments own Expert work and can reference that case-bound obligation.
- **Workbench and Gauntlet → RealityTrace and SEA-Forge.** Execution can yield observations and evidence. Acceptance or settlement is scoped to the criteria and authority that issued it.
- **Context Kernel → navigation and execution.** Citations supply context to a decision or run. Retrieval access and authority to act have separate owners.
- **SWE_SEED → SEA-Forge → GodSpeed-Agent.** Proof qualification can support an operational ruling. Developmental interpretation and capability promotion require their own evidence and judgment.
