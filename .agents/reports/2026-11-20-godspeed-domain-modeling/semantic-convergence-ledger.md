“Established” means a name we can use in specifications with the indicated meaning. “Proposed” identifies new language for a proven distinction where existing semantic or public labels still need owner review. “Open” requires a decision about authority or semantics. No entry authorizes changes to signatures, wire contracts, or evidence.
