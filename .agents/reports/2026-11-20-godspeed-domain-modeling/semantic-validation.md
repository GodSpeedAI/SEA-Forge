# Semantic validation

This review applies the inspected source invariants and existing test assertions to the proposed vocabulary. The scenarios are reasoning exercises. The convergence design and cross-process journeys require separate runtime evidence.

## Source-confirmed distinctions

- CognitiveOS v17 explicitly distinguishes the seven affordance conditions, non-fungible payment, external consequence registration and demonstrated Capability. The OCS decision interface can misclassify an apparent affordance. [Theory](source-evidence.md#t1-cognitiveos)
- DomainForge’s full identity digest, CEP scope, Horizon Closure Pin and Workbench Execution State Digest express different claims. Proving a binding requires source-specific checks. [DomainForge](source-evidence.md#d1-domainforge) · [Horizon](source-evidence.md#h1-horizon) · [Workbench](source-evidence.md#w1-cognate-workbench) · [SEA-Forge](source-evidence.md#s1-sea-forge)
- Case Assignment is a shared contract. Expert Assignment, Case-Step Correlation, Task and Role Invocation have different subjects and lifecycles. [Cognate](source-evidence.md#c1-cognate) · [Workbench](source-evidence.md#w1-cognate-workbench) · [Gauntlet](source-evidence.md#g1-gauntlet)
- Execution, verification, acceptance, receipt, interpretation and promotion have different evidence or authority. A universal status translation would lose these conditions. [CEP](source-evidence.md#p1-cep) · [SEA-Forge](source-evidence.md#s1-sea-forge) · [GodSpeed-Agent](source-evidence.md#a1-godspeed-agent)

## Decisions that remain open

1. **Horizon migration.** Adopt a typed World Reference for exchange. The owner decides whether to keep the closure-based startup check and approves the frozen contract migration.
2. **Theory and tier acceptance.** V17 and mathematical v2.3 retain their declared statuses. The theory owner decides when to freeze those projections. Product owners decide which Capability evidence must gate each deployment tier.
3. **Formal names and records.** Team Lead and Developmental Assessment are proposed terms for proven distinctions. Formal Workbench semantic role labels and GodSpeed-Agent event names require the applicable owner decision.
4. **E6 and CEP.** Both concern operational judgment. Their subjects, admissibility and finality differ. Consolidation would require evidence of common authority and a separate decision.

The established distinctions are ready for specification. Ticket generation that changes frozen models, public contracts or authority relies on the applicable owner decisions.
