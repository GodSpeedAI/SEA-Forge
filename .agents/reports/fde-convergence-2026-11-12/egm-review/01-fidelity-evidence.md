## Things the design gets right (do not lose)
1. Pre-registration of acceptance tests with hash-pinning before the first evaluated episode (G header, R-78).
2. Every NT has a named mutation that must turn it red (G §4), plus a clean control (E1) that must yield zero contradicted.
3. The one-unproven-critical-row rule makes the verdict NOT MET regardless of percentage (C §1); skipped means NOT TESTED.
4. G §7 keeps the bounded-mechanism claim distinct from reliability and transfer.
5. NT-05 covers denied, unavailable, expired and digest-mismatched decisions; NT-12 covers human-reserved decisions.
