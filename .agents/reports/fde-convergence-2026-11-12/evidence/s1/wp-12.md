## Limits / unverified
- Outermost binding only (`binding_depth: outermost`); quantifier must be the policy root, else `support: null`, no rows.
- Operation policies: precondition only, no `pre_state`, operation's declared actor used.
- Probe result: `exists_unique` with one true + one NULL gives policy `true` (existing eager expand counts only literal-true); binding rows show the NULL. Recorded in a test and docs, not changed.
- Not verified: multi-module models, role-bound policies, group_by, `state.*` policies, python/ts/wasm.
- Non-3VL graph mode: bindings use the boolean path; untested.
- Exit: 0 when evaluation ran; 2 on input/usage errors.
