# Screenshot-Backed UX Findings

## Affordance & Continuity Diagnosis
1. **Clear Action Paths**:
   - Primary affordances (e.g. "Create case", "Run preflight", "Commit case", "Recover outcome") are prominently displayed with accurate readiness badges.
2. **Governed Denial Surface**:
   - Guard evaluations render informative denial panels explaining specific blocked conditions (e.g., G9 readiness failure) rather than crashing or blanking the viewport.
3. **Responsive Shell**:
   - Shell and evidence drawer maintain non-overlapping layout at 1600x1000 viewport.
