# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Operators, reviewers and sponsors, and delegated agents doing governed work in the SEA Forge Workbench (Tauri 2 host + React 19 renderer).

Operator context: on-call or focused desk work, dim or mixed ambient light, 27-inch or laptop screen, one consequential run in focus. They check readiness, draft a case, pass preflight, commit, monitor execution, judge approvals, inspect evidence, and accept or reject settlement.

Reviewer and sponsor context: interrupted review flow. They arrive from the Inbox, need actor, exact action and resource, policy reason, evidence, expiry, and separation-of-duty warning, then approve or deny without blind batching.

Delegated agent context: bounded machine work behind the same authority fabric. Agent tasks are inspected as operations with role, endpoint, model, boundaries, turns, transcript evidence, termination reason, and settlement state, never as casual chat.

The job to be done: put one consequential operation behind an independent authority decision and still understand exactly what happened afterward. Govern with confidence.

## Product Purpose

SEA Forge is a runtime authority and evidence layer for autonomous systems. It evaluates proposed actions against declared domain meaning and policy before execution, returns allow, deny, or escalate decisions, and records evidence of what governed execution actually did.

The Workbench is the operational client of that kernel. It keeps navigation separate from mutation, draft separate from committed work, approval separate from execution, execution separate from settlement, agent termination separate from settlement, and settlement separate from capability.

Canonical journey: readiness to identity and authority to domain meaning to case draft to preflight to committed case to horizon to governed execution to evidence to settlement to capability and artifact maturity.

Success looks like: every screen answers where am I, what state is this in, why is it true, what evidence supports it, what can happen next, what authority or coordination is required, and what result would count as settlement. One governed focus and one dominant lawful action per screen. No path is spendable without explanation.

## Brand Personality

Rigorous, calm under pressure, source-grounded. Operational, not promotional. Cybernetic, not dopamine-driven.

Voice is calm operational precision. Short declarative sentences that name the state domain and the next lawful path. Evidence earns belief. Precision signals authority.

GodSpeed house language applies (see `godspeed-growth/brand/visual/visual-language.md`): institutional control plane meets cinematic technical editorial. Serious infrastructure with controlled velocity. Restraint over decoration, system signals over AI spectacle, spendable clarity over grandiosity. Enterprise register by default (heavier, calmer, formal), AI-native sharpness only when attention demands it and still evidence-disciplined.

Reference the feel of Linear precision and density, Raycast command-driven recognition, Bloomberg Terminal information density without card soup, NASA and SpaceX console focus with supporting context around it, Palantir operational gravity without heaviness, Vercel infrastructure clarity. Gold behaves as proof mark or authority signal, never ornament.

## Anti-references

What this should NOT look like:

Strict bans from current system: Jira, Monday, Asana, ClickUp card soup and oversized analytics dashboards. Consumer chat apps as the agent metaphor. Marketing heroes inside the product. Generic green SUCCESS badges that collapse authority, execution, settlement, integrity, and capability into one meaning. Color-only status. Hidden disabled paths, blind batch approval, invented Recommended or Safe labels, invented scores or pricing, invented CLI commands or backend states. Draft views that imply commitment. Execution output presented as settlement truth. Drag and drop treated as authoritative mutation.

Brand bans from GodSpeed visual language: glowing AI brain, humanoid robots, neon on black crypto or gaming contrast, particle fields, glowing holograms, circuit-board stock tech, floating node graphs, gear-and-cog automation icons, futuristic skylines, rocket or explosion acceleration without governance, bridge or construction infrastructure clichés, luxury gold ornamentation, flat generic SaaS blue, warm lifestyle beige, friendly pastels. Social avatars and favicons use icon-only mark. Logo never stretched, beveled, glowed, or placed on busy backgrounds.

## Design Principles

1. Authority before action. Nothing consequential executes without a prior allow. Unknown means deny. Denial and escalation are first-class results with reasons and repair paths, not missing states.
2. Settlement is not execution. Exit zero is evidence, not acceptance. Execution, dialogue termination, settlement, integrity, and capability each keep their own label, icon, placement, and tone.
3. One focus, one lawful action. The center holds the current governed object. Secondary context lives in rails and drawers. Unavailable actions show reason and nearest lawful repair within one action of the state.
4. Evidence within reach, not underneath. Source, freshness, provenance, and verification ride with the state they explain. Stale views retain prior verified state but never present it as current.
5. Calm under consequence. Dense but legible, keyboard-first, reduced-motion safe. Motion signals a governed transition only. Accepted states stay quiet. No celebration, no blame, no vague confidence.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Keyboard traversal through all primary-path actions with visible focus rings. Tables with proper semantics for matrices, list alternative for every graph, 200 percent zoom without state or action loss.

Color is redundant, never sole carrier. Every state pairs label plus icon or shape plus stable placement plus accessible description. Restrict low-contrast tertiary text to non-essential or large text. Live announcements only for meaningful governed transitions. Honor prefers-reduced-motion by collapsing transitions to near zero.
