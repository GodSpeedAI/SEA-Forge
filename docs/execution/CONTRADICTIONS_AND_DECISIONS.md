## Bounded Deferrals

- Real ACP, SWE_SEED, witness, IFL, and macOS Seatbelt release evidence may be
  skipped only when the corresponding optional integration or platform is not
  part of the artifact under test. The skip and prerequisite must remain visible.
- Full catalog breadth, remote clients, HTTP, multi-user authentication,
  containers, Windows, MicroVM, NATS, and hosted deployment are not required for
  the local Linux/macOS product.
- Registry publication of every internal crate is deferred. Binary distribution
  is sufficient for the first supported install.
