# SEA-Forge Governance and Settlement

SEA-Forge governs consequential operations and records their authority, execution, evidence, and settlement. Its case model organizes governed work around an intent and a case plan.

## Language

### Case management

**Managed Case**:
A governed unit of work founded on an intent and carried out through a case plan. It can contain multiple execution episodes.
_Avoid_: Case (in cross-product discussion)

**Case Plan**:
The authority-checkable structure of a managed case, including its plan items and the conditions under which their work proceeds.

**Plan Item**:
A unit of planned work within a managed case that can become eligible for execution and has its own outcome conditions.

**Sentry**:
An event and optional condition that together govern a plan item's eligibility for a transition.

**Intent**:
An attributed statement of the transformation an operator seeks.

**Execution Episode**:
One execution of a plan item or task within SEA-Forge's governance lifecycle.

**Cell**:
A local unit of SEA-Forge deployment and governance with its own operational state.

### Authority

**Actor**:
The human, agent, service, or system represented as requesting or performing an operation.

**Principal**:
The identity resolved by the authority boundary for evaluating an actor's standing.

**Authority Decision**:
A ruling on whether a particular actor may perform a specified operation under the applicable policy and constraints.

**Action Grant**:
A single-use authorization for a specific action within its approved limits.

### Evidence and settlement

**Evidence Record**:
An attributable account of an artifact, authority decision, or execution result that can be considered in evaluating a claim.

**Pre-Mint Identity**:
The provisional identity of an evidenced work product and its attribution before external attestation or capitalization.

**Settlement Criteria**:
Conditions declared before execution against which the claimed outcome will be judged.

**Settlement Claim**:
The proposed outcome and supporting facts submitted for judgment under declared settlement criteria.

**Settlement Evaluation**:
The kernel's finding on whether a claim meets predeclared criteria, with the basis for the finding.

**Settlement Basis**:
The contributing facts and checks that explain a settlement evaluation or ruling.

**Settlement Declaration**:
A settlement authority's attributable ruling on a specific claim and its evidentiary basis, with stated standing and strength.

**Operational Settlement**:
A ruling by the responsible authority about the outcome of a particular governed operation under its declared criteria and admissible evidence. The ruling records its disposition and whether it is final.
_Avoid_: Completion, developmental settlement

**Settlement Receipt**:
An attributable record of a settlement ruling, bound to the issuing authority, the subject judged, and the ruling’s status and finality.

### Delegated case work

**Case Assignment**:
One issued attempt to carry out a specific case step under a pinned episode, delegated authority, and a budget tracked across distinct currencies.
_Avoid_: Assignment (unqualified), Expert Assignment
